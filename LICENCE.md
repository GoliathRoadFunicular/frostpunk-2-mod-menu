## 🧩 About

**Frostpunk 2 Companion** is a curated list of Frostpunk 2 open-source tools:

- **Overlays** — on-screen helpers, layout notes, resource viewer, heat map notes
- **Quality-of-Life Helpers** — city planner notes, faction tracker, policy overview
- **Research Notes** — survival layouts and config notes for educational use

Based on publicly available community tools and overlay helpers.

---

## ✨ Features

### 🎮 City Management Helpers
- Resource Viewer — track coal, food, materials, and heat at a glance
- Heat Map Notes — visualize district warmth and generator load
- Population Tracker — review worker and citizen distribution
- Time Control Notes — adjust simulation pacing for study (0.5x to 3x)

### 🏛️ Faction & Policy Notes
- Faction Overview — browse faction moods and tension notes
- Policy Catalog — organize researched laws and social paths
- Council Notes — review voting outcomes and proposals
- Frostland Map Notes — plan expedition routes and supply lines

### 🏋️ Planning Layout
- Build Planner Notes — sketch district layouts on any map
- Generator Layout Notes — compare heat zone configurations
- Resource Chain Notes — map production and storage flows
